# Revenue Command Center v1.1 — Hospital Procurement Demo

## Demo environment
A synthetic-data hospital environment for demonstrating the complete revenue-cycle workflow.

## Demo script
1. Sign in as Owner.
2. Show the executive dashboard.
3. Open a high-priority denial.
4. Assign the claim.
5. Prepare an appeal.
6. Show the claim timeline/audit trail.
7. Review the appeal queue.
8. Demonstrate payer-connection placeholders.
9. Show the administration/security-readiness checklist.

## Procurement positioning
Position the product as a revenue-cycle workflow and analytics platform that can integrate with authorized hospital, clearinghouse, EHR, and payer systems.

Do not claim HIPAA certification or production interoperability until those have actually been implemented, assessed, contracted, and tested.

## CMS opportunity
CMS says impacted payers must implement specified Prior Authorization APIs beginning January 1, 2027, with certain operational provisions generally beginning January 1, 2026. The Prior Authorization API requirements include documentation requirements and responses indicating approval, denial with a specific reason, or a request for more information. Exact applicability varies by payer type.

## Security gate
Before production PHI, complete a formal security/risk program, appropriate agreements/BAAs where applicable, strong authentication and authorization, encryption, centralized audit logging, secure document storage, backup/restore, vulnerability management, penetration testing, incident response, retention/deletion controls, and authorized integrations.
