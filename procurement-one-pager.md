# Revenue Command Center — One-Page Concept

### Problem
Revenue-cycle teams often move between denial lists, authorization records, payer rules, spreadsheets, email, and status reports.

### Solution
A centralized command center for:
- denial intake and classification
- authorization verification
- payer rule management
- specialist assignment
- appeal preparation
- deadline alerts
- executive reporting
- auditability

### Pilot
Validate the workflow with synthetic/de-identified records before production integration.

### Integration roadmap
EHR/clearinghouse inputs → Revenue Command Center → authorized payer/authorization APIs → workflow → outcome analytics.

### Important
This is a product concept and pilot software, not a compliance certification, legal opinion, or guarantee of recovered revenue.
